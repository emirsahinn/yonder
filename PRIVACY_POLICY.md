# Yonder Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Yonder is a focus and work tracking app. This policy explains what information Yonder uses and why.

## Information We Collect

Yonder may store:

- Focus session duration, date, start time, end time, and selected work area.
- Work areas created by the user.
- Goal and reminder preferences.
- Profile name, profile image, and Google email if the user connects a Google account.
- Online room participation details such as room code, display name, status, duration, and timestamps.

## How We Use Information

We use this information to:

- Track focus history and reports.
- Sync work areas, goals, sessions, and rooms across devices when a Google account is connected.
- Show reminders and weekly summaries.
- Support online focus rooms.
- Unlock and manage Yonder PRO features through Apple in-app purchases.

## Account Sync

Yonder can be used locally. If the user connects a Google account, selected app data may be synced with Firebase services so it can appear on other devices signed into the same account.

## Payments

Yonder PRO purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Yonder does not receive or store full payment card details.

## Notifications

Yonder may send local notifications for reminders and weekly summaries if the user grants notification permission. Notification settings can be changed in iOS Settings.

## Photos

If the user selects a profile photo, Yonder uses the iOS photo picker. The selected image may be stored locally and synced if account sync is enabled.

## Data Sharing

Yonder does not sell user data and does not use third-party advertising trackers.

Yonder uses Firebase and Google Sign-In for authentication, sync, and online room functionality.

## Data Deletion

Users can permanently delete their account and all stored data directly within the app by navigating to **Settings → Danger Zone → Delete My Account and Data**.

When account deletion is triggered:
- All cloud data under `users/{uid}` (sessions, work areas, goals) is permanently erased from Firebase Firestore.
- Online room participation records are removed and hosted active rooms are ended.
- The Firebase Authentication user account is deleted.
- All local device data (sessions, work areas, goals, reminders, and local profile metadata) is wiped.

For additional support or data deletion inquiries, users can contact support at info@yonderfocusapp.com.

## Contact

For privacy questions or support requests, please contact info@yonderfocusapp.com.
