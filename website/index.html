Yours

Your data starts on your device.

Yonder works fully offline. Connect a Google account only if you want your sessions, work areas, and goals to sync across devices.

  • No ad trackers, no data sold, ever
  • Sync is optional and off by default
  • Delete your account and all data from inside the app, any time
What exactly does Yonder store? →

Focus session duration, timing, and work area; work areas you create; goal and reminder preferences; and, if you connect a Google account, your profile name, photo, and email so your data can sync.

Online focus rooms store room code, display name, and status only for the duration of the session.

Questions

Clear answers before you start.

Is Yonder free?+

Yes. The timer, up to 10 work areas, focus rooms, Live Activity, weekly reports, reminders, and sync are free. Yonder PRO adds unlimited work areas and goal tracking.

Do I need an account?+

No. Yonder works fully signed out. Connect a Google account only if you want your sessions synced across devices.

Can I focus alongside friends?+

Yes — create or join a focus room with a six-character code. Everyone in the room runs their timer together, in real time.

Does the timer keep going if I leave the app?+

Yes. A Live Activity keeps counting down on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island until the session ends.

What does Yonder run on?+

iPhone and iPad, iOS 26 or later.

How do I delete my data?+

Settings → Danger Zone → Delete My Account and Data, right inside the app. It permanently removes your cloud and local data.
